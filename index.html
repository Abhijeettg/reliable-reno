FAQ

Good questions to ask a builder.

Can't see yours? Call or send us a message and we'll answer it straight.

How much does a renovation cost?

It depends on size, finishes and what's behind the walls. That's why we visit first: after the site visit you get a fixed, itemised quote, so you know the full price before any work starts.

How much deposit do you take?

Under NSW law the deposit for residential building work is capped at 10% of the contract price. After that you pay in stages as work is completed, as set out in your contract.

Do I need council approval?

Many internal renovations don't. Extensions and structural work usually need a Development Application (DA) or a Complying Development Certificate (CDC). We'll tell you which applies at the site visit and can manage the approval for you.

How long will it take?

As a rough guide, a bathroom takes 3-4 weeks and a kitchen 4-6 weeks once work starts. Extensions vary more. You'll get a written schedule with your quote.

Can we keep living at home during the work?

Usually, yes. We plan the job to keep at least one bathroom and the kitchen usable where possible, and we clean up at the end of every day.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold a NSW contractor licence and public liability insurance, and jobs over $20,000 are covered by the Home Building Compensation Fund, as NSW law requires.
